# Datasets

The repository contains three datasets with different roles.

## 1. B-READY seed package: `Data/raw/bready_dataset_v2.zip`

A source-derived package built around the World Bank B-READY 2024 Business Entry topic (schema and provenance in
`Data/raw/DATASET_SCHEMA.md`): 50 economies, 18 services, 9 resources, 7 requirements, 5 constraints, 16 fragments,
17 hyperedges, 250 controlled requests and repository scales of 10-50 economies. It is the seed from which the main
repository is generated; it is not used directly by the experiments. `hypergraph.py` extracts it into `Data/seed/`
when needed.

## 2. Main repository: `Data/main/` (used by all experiments)

Generated by `Data/build_base.py` (`python hypergraph.py --rebuild`) from the seed package and the domain lexicon
(`hfc/lex.py`); the generation is deterministic and reproduces the shipped files byte for byte.

| Item | Count |
|---|---:|
| Economies (B-READY 2024) | 50 |
| Procedure domains (permits, registrations, licences, company formation, benefits, documents) | 65 |
| Procedures (economy x domain) | 3,250 |
| Fragments | 29,300 |
| Hyperedges | 52,400 (6,500 SEQ, 22,750 AND, 22,750 XOR, 400 LOOP) |

Every procedure instantiates a common nine-fragment template (`hfc/build.py`, `make_procedure`): eligibility for a
first application or a renewal (XOR variant group), documents, fee, two optional additional-document variants,
standard or priority appointment (XOR) and submission, preceded by an economy entry fragment. Procedures differ in
their descriptions, economy, statutory delays, validity windows and in a quality factor derived from the economy's
B-READY pillar score. The Business Entry layers of the seed are kept as reference tables (`seed_*.json`,
`seed_pillar_scores_2024.csv`). `manifest.json` lists the counts and the SHA-256 checksums of the files.

Repositories of size |F| < 29,300 are obtained by sampling whole procedures (`hfc/repo.py`), most frequent
procedures first (`hot_procedures` in the manifest).

## 3. Demonstration prompts and test fixtures: `Prompts/`, `Test/`

`Prompts/prompts.json` holds 5 hand-written requests served by `python HyFragComp.py --prompts`; `Test/first_test.py`
builds a small scenario. They illustrate the pipeline and are not used by the experiments.

## Workloads

Request streams and change events are generated for each run, not stored:

- `Data/build_requests.py`: request texts from procedure blocks, with personal literals, paraphrases (`unseen_p`),
  distracting sentences and the six request categories (identical, priority-flipped, qualifier-flipped, extended,
  reduced, unrelated) in the proportions of `streams.mix`;
- `Data/build_records.py`: the stored composition records of the warm start;
- `Data/build_events.py`: expiries, withdrawals and non-substitutable revisions (proportions `streams.ev_mix`) plus
  substitutable revisions (`streams.subst_frac`), applied to a fraction pi_st of the fragments that support the
  stored records (`stale` in `build_stream`, `streams.stale_default` otherwise) and spread over the stream;
- `Data/inst.py`: assembles a stream (`build_stream`) from these generators for a given seed.

All generator parameters are in `configs/default.yaml` (`streams` and `experiments`).
